Questions

Good questions to ask before you start.

Will my customers know they're talking to EZER?

We reply in your business's name and tone, following guidelines you approve. Whether you introduce us as part of your team is entirely your choice.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. All plans are month-to-month, so you can upgrade, downgrade or stop as your business changes.

What do you need from me to get started?

Access to the channels you'd like us to cover (for example WhatsApp Business and email), your price list and common questions, and a short consultation call.

Which channels do you cover?

WhatsApp and email as standard. Other channels can be discussed during your consultation.

Who owns my customer data?

You do. We only use your customer information to serve your customers on your behalf.

What if I get more enquiries than my plan covers?

We'll let you know as you approach your limit and recommend whether upgrading makes sense. You stay in control.

Which languages do you reply in?

English, Bahasa Malaysia and Tamil, matched to how your customers message you.

What hours do you cover?

We work on Malaysia time (GMT+8) and treat speed as the priority: every enquiry is answered promptly and followed up on schedule. Your exact coverage hours are agreed during the consultation.

EZER is new. Why should I trust you with my customers?

You're right, we're newly launched, so we don't have public case studies yet. What you get is Kenisha's years of hands-on experience in sales, client care and CRM, reply guidelines you approve before we go live, and month-to-month plans so you're never locked in.

Will EZER replace my sales team?

No. We handle the admin around sales (replies, follow-ups and records) so your team can spend more time closing.
